# Today Photos Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 30, 2026

Today Photos is designed to process your photo library on your Apple device. The developer does not operate an account system, analytics service, advertising service, tracking service, photo server, or other service that collects data from the app.

## Photos Access

With your permission, Today Photos uses Apple's PhotoKit framework to find photos whose capture month and day match the date you are viewing. The app reads photo dates, identifiers, image data, and available capture metadata only to provide its gallery, photo detail, sharing, and Home Screen widget features. Today Photos does not modify or delete your photo library.

If an item is stored in iCloud Photos, Apple's PhotoKit system service may download it according to your Photos and iCloud settings. That transfer is provided by Apple, not by a developer-operated server.

## On-Device Storage

Today Photos creates resized JPEG thumbnails and small cache manifests in the app's local shared container so its WidgetKit extension can display photos while the main app is not running. The widget extension reads this prepared cache; it does not independently scan your photo library. Cache files remain on the device and are managed by the app and iOS.

## Photo Details and Maps

When a photo contains location metadata, Today Photos may use Apple MapKit and geocoding system services to display a map and a human-readable place name. Apple may process the coordinates under Apple's terms and privacy policy. Today Photos does not send photo locations to a developer-operated service.

## Sharing

Sharing is always initiated by you. When you tap Share, Today Photos asks PhotoKit for the original or best available full-size photo and passes a temporary local copy to Apple's system share sheet. The destination you choose processes the shared item under that destination's own terms and privacy policy.

## Data Collection

The developer does not collect data from Today Photos. In particular, the app does not collect or transmit photos, photo metadata, precise location, contact information, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics, or advertising data to the developer or third-party partners.

If you contact support, you may choose to provide an email address, screenshots, logs, or other information. That information is used only to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue.

## Your Choices

You can change or revoke Photos access in iOS or iPadOS Settings. You can also remove the app to delete its local app container and widget caches, subject to normal Apple device backup behavior.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or legal requirements change. The effective date above will be revised when changes are published.
