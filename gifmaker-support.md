# GIFMaker Support

GIFMaker turns videos from Photos or Files into looping GIFs on iPhone and iPad. It requires iOS 26 or iPadOS 26 or later.

## Create a GIF

1. Open GIFMaker and choose a video from **Photos** or **Files**.
2. In **Video**, drag the ends of the filmstrip to trim. Drag within the selected range to scrub, or tap Play to preview the loop.
3. Use **Crop** to frame the subject or **Caption** to add text. The **Precise Trim** and **Fine-tune Crop** controls give you slider alternatives to gestures.
4. Tap **Done**, choose a quality setting, and tap **Create GIF**. The file-size estimate can change with motion and detail.
5. In **Preview**, tap **Save to Photos** to keep a copy in your library or **Share GIF** to send it or save it to Files. The preview shows the measured file size and dimensions.

## Troubleshooting

- **A video won’t open.** If the original is stored in iCloud or with another file provider, download it to the device first. For a compatibility issue, try exporting a local MP4 or MOV from the source app.
- **The GIF is too large.** Return to editing and trim to a shorter moment. Choose **Smaller File** in Export Settings or expand **Advanced** to adjust the size target. Compare the measured size in Preview with your destination’s limit.
- **Save to Photos is blocked.** Check GIFMaker’s Photos access in Settings. You can also use **Share GIF** and choose **Save to Files**.
- **The preview is still.** Tap **Play Animation**. GIFMaker starts the preview paused when Reduce Motion is on.
- **An export was interrupted.** Keep GIFMaker open while it creates the GIF. If the export is canceled, return to the editor and tap **Done** to try again. Save or share a copy before choosing **Start Another GIF**.

GIFMaker creates GIFs on the device. Once the original video is local, creating a GIF works offline. Photos and Files may use your configured Apple or third-party storage services when you choose a cloud item or destination.
