# Privacy Policy for Mac App Store Apps

**Last updated:** 2026-05-19

This privacy policy applies to macOS applications distributed by nibrwr through the Mac App Store.

## Information Collected
The app does not intentionally collect, store, or transmit personal information.

Information you enter into the app, create with the app, or store through the app remains on your device unless you choose to share, export, sync, or otherwise transfer it using macOS, iCloud, or another service.

## How Information Is Used
Because the app does not intentionally collect personal information, it does not use personal information for advertising, profiling, tracking, or analytics.

Any data you provide within the app is used only to provide the app's functionality on your device.

## Data Storage and Retention
- App data is stored locally on your Mac unless you choose to use system features or third-party services to move it elsewhere.
- The app does not maintain a server-side user account or database for your personal information.
- Removing the app may not automatically remove all documents or files you created or stored outside the app container.

## Data Sharing
The app does not sell, rent, or share user data with third parties.

If you use Apple services such as the Mac App Store, iCloud, crash reporting, or App Analytics, Apple may process information under Apple's privacy policies and your device settings.

## Diagnostics and Support
If you contact support, you may choose to provide information such as your email address, screenshots, logs, or a description of the issue. That information is used only to respond to your request and troubleshoot the issue.

## Children's Privacy
The app is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through a support request, contact us so it can be deleted.

## Changes to This Policy
This policy may be updated from time to time. Changes will be posted at the same location with an updated date.
